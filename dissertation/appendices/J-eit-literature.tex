\noindent\textit{This appendix documents the corpus of peer-reviewed academic literature on the European Institute of Innovation and Technology (EIT) ecosystem, compiled via Scopus search in January 2025.}

\smallskip

\noindent\textbf{Search query:}
\begin{quote}
\small\texttt{TITLE-ABS-KEY("European Institute of Innovation and Technology" OR "EIT KIC" OR "Knowledge and Innovation Community")}
\end{quote}

\noindent\textit{This corpus demonstrates the relative scarcity of academic research on this major EU policy experiment---an observation that motivates this dissertation's empirical contribution. Titles link to DOIs where available.}
