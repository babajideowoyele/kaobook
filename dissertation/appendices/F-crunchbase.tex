\chapter{Crunchbase: Mapping Investment Networks}
\label{app:crunchbase}
%************************************************

\noindent\textit{This appendix examines Crunchbase as a data source for studying innovation ecosystems, particularly startup-driven sustainability transitions. We explore the platform's unique ability to map inter-organizational networks through investment, acquisition, and founder relationships. The appendix addresses data extraction methods, analytical techniques, and integration strategies while acknowledging critical limitations around coverage bias and reproducibility.}

\bigskip


%═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
\section*{Overview: Scope, Strengths, and Limitations}
%═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

\textbf{Crunchbase} is a prominent online platform compiling information on innovative companies (especially startups), the people behind them, and their funding activities.

%\sidenote{\textbf{Platform Scale (2024):} 1M+ organizations, 199 countries, hundreds of thousands of people and investment records.}

Since its creation in 2007, it has grown into one of the largest directories of startups worldwide, providing detailed information not easily obtainable from official sources.

\subsection*{Data Assembly Model}

Crunchbase's dataset is \textbf{community- and partner-driven}:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*, itemsep=0.2em]
\item Registered users (entrepreneurs, executives) create/update profiles
\item Partnerships with ~3,000 investment firms for portfolio updates
\item ML-augmented verification
\item In-house data analysts
\end{itemize}


%═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
\section*{Data Richness: Relational Structure}
%═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Unlike patent databases or news archives, Crunchbase explicitly encodes \textbf{relationships}:

\begin{description}[leftmargin=0.5cm, itemsep=0.3em]
\item[Funding Relationships] Which investors funded which companies, amounts, and rounds
\item[Acquisition Relationships] Which companies acquired which others
\item[People Relationships] Founders, executives, board members across companies
\item[Accelerator Relationships] Which programs supported which startups
\end{description}

%\sidenote{\textbf{Network Construction:} Crunchbase data naturally generates bipartite networks (investor-company) and one-mode projections (co-investor networks).}


%═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
\section*{Analytical Applications}
%═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

\subsection*{Investment Network Analysis}

Funding data enables construction of investor-startup networks revealing:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*, itemsep=0.2em]
\item Central vs. peripheral investors
\item Sector specialization patterns
\item Geographic concentration
\item Syndication patterns (co-investment)
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{Founder Network Analysis}

Serial entrepreneurs and board members create ties between organizations:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*, itemsep=0.2em]
\item Knowledge transfer pathways
\item Entrepreneurial communities
\item Spin-off dynamics
\end{itemize}


%═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
\section*{Critical Limitations}
%═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

\begin{criticalbox}[Coverage and Bias Issues]
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*, itemsep=0.2em]
\item \textbf{US/Tech bias}: North American and technology sectors overrepresented
\item \textbf{Survivorship bias}: Failed companies underreported
\item \textbf{Self-reporting}: Data quality depends on user contributions
\item \textbf{Recency bias}: Historical data less complete
\item \textbf{Access barriers}: Full API access requires commercial subscription
\end{itemize}
\end{criticalbox}


%═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
\section*{Chapter Summary}
%═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

\begin{summarybox}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*, itemsep=0.3em]
\item \textbf{Crunchbase} provides rich relational data on startups, investors, and their connections.

\item \textbf{Network construction} is natural: funding rounds create investor-company ties; people create company-company ties.

\item \textbf{Analytical applications} include investment network analysis, founder mobility tracking, and ecosystem mapping.

\item \textbf{Critical limitations} include geographic/sectoral bias, survivorship bias, and data access barriers.

\item \textbf{Integration with other modalities} strengthens validity by triangulating investment patterns with claimed identities and media coverage.
\end{itemize}
\end{summarybox}
