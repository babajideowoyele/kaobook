\chapter{Glossary of Terms}
\labch{appendix-glossary}

\begin{description}[leftmargin=0.5cm, style=nextline]

\item[Complex Adaptive System (CAS)] A system composed of many interacting agents whose aggregate behavior emerges from local interactions rather than central control.

\item[Interstitial Pluralism] The phenomenon where actors occupy in-between positions across multiple role categories simultaneously.

\item[Knowledge Triangle] The EIT's conceptual framework integrating education, research, and business/innovation.

\item[Multimodal Cartography] The methodological approach of mapping phenomena using multiple complementary data sources.

\item[Role Constellation] The dynamic configuration of interconnected roles within an innovation ecosystem.

\item[\RoleBox] The computational toolkit developed in this dissertation for multimodal role constellation analysis.

\item[\RoleSim] The agent-based modeling platform for simulating role constellation dynamics.

\end{description}
