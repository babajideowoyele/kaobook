\noindent\textit{Propositions pertaining to the dissertation}

\vspace{0.8cm}

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.5cm, itemsep=0.8em, label=\textbf{\Roman*.}]

\item \textbf{Role constellations, not individual roles, are the appropriate unit of analysis for understanding actor contributions to sustainability transitions.}\\
\textit{This dissertation, passim.}

\item \textbf{Multimodal cartography reveals systematic discrepancies between how organizations present themselves and how they are positioned by others—discrepancies that single-modality approaches cannot detect.}\\
\textit{Chapters 5--9.}

\item \textbf{The claim to ``bridge'' or ``connect'' is the most inflated role in innovation ecosystems: 34\% of actors claim connector roles, but only 12\% show cross-modal evidence for actually performing them.}\\
\textit{Chapter 5.}

\item \textbf{Design Science Research provides a productive framework for methodological development in sustainability transitions research, treating methods themselves as artifacts subject to iterative refinement.}\\
\textit{Chapter 3.}

\item \textbf{Computational methods applied to transitions research generate valuable findings when grounded in domain theory; without such grounding, they risk producing sophisticated analyses of what can be measured rather than what matters.}\\
\textit{Chapter 1.}

\item \textbf{The visual presentation of organizations—logos, color schemes, imagery—constitutes an underexplored but information-rich modality for understanding role positioning in innovation ecosystems.}\\
\textit{Chapter 8.}

\item \textbf{Power in innovation ecosystems operates less through direct control than through the capacity to define roles and position others within constellations.}\\
\textit{Chapter 9.}

\item \textbf{Open-source research infrastructure should be the default in computational social science; proprietary pipelines undermine cumulative knowledge building.}\\
\textit{Appendix H.}

\item \textbf{The European Institute of Innovation and Technology (EIT) ecosystem represents an unprecedented natural experiment in designed transitions governance, worthy of sustained scholarly attention.}\\
\textit{Intermezzo: EIT Communities.}

\item \textbf{Agent-based models of role dynamics serve their greatest value not in prediction but in disciplining counterfactual imagination.}\\
\textit{Intermezzo: RoleSim.}

\end{enumerate}
