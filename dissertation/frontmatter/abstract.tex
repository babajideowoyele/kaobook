This dissertation develops a multimodal cartography of role constellations in sustainability transitions, with empirical focus on the European Institute of Innovation and Technology (EIT) ecosystem. Drawing on Complex Adaptive Systems theory and Design Science Research methodology, we introduce \RoleBox---a computational toolkit for mapping how actors position themselves and are positioned by others across multiple data modalities.

\textbf{Keywords:} role constellations, innovation ecosystems, sustainability transitions, multimodal analysis, complex adaptive systems, EIT
