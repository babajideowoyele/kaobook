\section*{Reading the Metaframework}

\begin{description}[leftmargin=2cm, style=nextline, itemsep=0.2em]
\item[\textcolor{DarkGreen}{\faDatabase\ Common Inputs}]
All empirical chapters begin with the same entity master list (2,192 EIT organizations across 8 KICs) and theoretical anchors (Knowledge Triangle, X-Curve, role theory).

\item[\textcolor{Slate}{\faStream\ Workflow Stages}]
Each lane follows Maass et al.'s bridging framework: curation (data collection), annotation (theory-guided coding), unsupervised learning (pattern discovery), and outputs. Stage ordering varies by modality.

\item[\textcolor{AccentPurple}{\faObjectGroup\ Cross-Modal Triangulation}]
Chapter 10 synthesizes patterns across all five modalities, identifying systematic gaps (connector gap, build-up bias) and recurring structures (sociotypes).

\item[\textcolor{PandaCharcoal}{\faServer\ Open Infrastructure}]
All pipelines, data, and analysis tools are released as open-source RoleBox modules, enabling replication and extension.
\end{description}
